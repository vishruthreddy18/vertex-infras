Why This Location

Vertex Vihanth sits in Muthsandra, near Varthur, Bengaluru, with strong connectivity to the city's key employment and lifestyle hubs.

  • IT parks within 8-15 km: ITPL, Ecospace, Oracle Tech Hub, Sigma Soft Tech Park, Wipro
  • Direct access via Sarjapura Main Road to Electronic City, and Varthur to Whitefield & ITPL via the Business Corridor Road
  • Metro connectivity: Whitefield Metro today, with the Sarjapura Road Metro coming soon
  • Reputed schools nearby: National Public School, The Foundation School, Greenwood High, and more
  • Healthcare access via Manipal Hospital (Varthur Kodi) and upcoming facilities in Carmelaram

The Project

  • 182 apartments across 14 floors on 2.20 acres
  • E-Khata land, Hoskote BMRDA & RERA approved
  • Choice of 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes
  • 9M & 7.5M wide concrete roads with footpaths; all utilities (storm water, sewage, electrical) laid underground
