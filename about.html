About Us

The people and principles behind every Vertex home

Building homes in Bengaluru for over 30 years

Vertex Infras is a Bengaluru-based developer of premium villas and apartments. Over three decades we have delivered 20+ projects, from gated villa communities in Varthur to apartment homes off Sarjapur Road.

Our roots are in engineering. Alongside our own residential projects, we have carried out civil and infrastructure works for clients including GMR Group, GVK and Bangalore International Airport, and that same discipline goes into every home we build.
