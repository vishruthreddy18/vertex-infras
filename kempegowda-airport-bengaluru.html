Bangalore International Airport

Bengaluru  ·  2022

Completed

Overview

Vertex Infras carried out road construction and infrastructure development works for the nursery unit at Bangalore International Airport (Kempegowda International Airport), Bengaluru. The scope covered internal road network construction, site grading, drainage, and supporting infrastructure for the airport's nursery and green-cover facility.

Highlights

  • Internal road network construction
  • Site grading & earthworks
  • Drainage & stormwater systems for the nursery unit
  • Supporting infrastructure for landscaping & green cover
  • Works executed within live airport premises
  • Delivered to aviation-grade safety standards
